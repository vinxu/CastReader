# CastReader 商店更新说明

适用版本：iOS 1.2.9 (19)、Android 1.0.11 (15)

## 中文

- 新增 18 个多语言音色：日语 5 个、西班牙语 3 个、法语 1 个、巴西葡萄牙语 3 个、意大利语 2 个、印地语 4 个。
- 音色列表新增语言筛选，并默认选择与你更相关的语言，查找音色更轻松。
- 改进朗读、解读、内容导入和订阅流程的稳定性与性能。

## English

- Added 18 multilingual voices: 5 Japanese, 3 Spanish, 1 French, 3 Brazilian Portuguese, 2 Italian, and 4 Hindi.
- Added language filtering for voices, with a relevant language selected by default so voices are easier to find.
- Improved reliability and performance across reading, Explain, content imports, and subscriptions.
